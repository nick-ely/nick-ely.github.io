EPS disc cutting • motion study

Stationary vertical wire · rotating and translating foam · eight blocking pockets

Illustrative kinematics, not an exact CAD/follower simulation. Assumes 42″ core, 48″ square stock, 4″ thickness, eight radial tapered pockets approximately 1¾″ wide × 1¾″ deep. Wire diameter, kerf, thermal lag, follower offset and clearances are not modeled. Playback is accelerated and does not predict cutting time. Rotation pauses during radial pocket entry/exit; pocket floors follow short arcs.
