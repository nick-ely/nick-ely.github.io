EPS disc cutting • track & follower motion study

Stationary wire and follower pin · disc rotates on the sliding table · the cam track drives the table in and out

Drag to orbit · right-drag to pan · scroll to zoom

Illustrative kinematics, not an exact CAD simulation. Assumes 42″ disc from 48″ square × 4″ EPS stock, eight radial pockets 1¾″ wide × 1¾″ deep, and 40 mm (1.575″) extrusion. The base crossmembers are 26.750″. Wire and follower pin are fixed on the same line of travel, 10″ apart, so the track is the cut profile pulled in 10″ radially (same angles, same pocket depth). Wire diameter, kerf, pin and groove clearance, and thermal lag are not modeled. Rotation pauses while the pin runs the radial walls of a pocket. Playback is accelerated and does not predict cutting time.
